# Choir / Voices

Human voices with no words. The most emotionally direct sound available and the
one that most easily becomes a cliché, because it does the work of the music
before the music has done any.

## Sound

- **Source:** the `pad` voice, formant-shaped. Vowel choice is the whole
  expression: **ooh** is distant and cold, **aah** is present and warm, and the
  difference between a threat and a benediction is often only that.
- **Signal chain:** compression → a long hall → widening. Voices without a room
  are uncanny; there is no such thing as a close-miked choir.
- **Character:** breath before the note, and never a clean start. If the attack is
  instant, it is an organ.

## When to reach for it

[`solemn`](../emotion/solemn.md) above all, then [`epic`](../emotion/epic.md),
[`mysterious`](../emotion/mysterious.md) and
[`spaghetti-western`](../genre/spaghetti-western.md), where the wordless soprano
is a defining sound rather than a decoration.

## How to voice it

Parallel fifths and octaves, moving slowly, in the middle of the register — see
[`solemn`](../emotion/solemn.md) on organum. Four voices maximum. Do not give a
choir a fast line: the reason it reads as human is that humans breathe, and a
sixteenth-note run says otherwise immediately.
