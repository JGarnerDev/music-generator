# Disco

Four-on-the-floor with a backbeat *and* an open hat — house's ancestor, played by
people. The difference from house is that everything above the drums is busy:
a sixteenth-note guitar chank, an octave-jumping bass, and strings.

## Groove

- **Tempo:** 108–125 BPM.
- **The bass line is the hook.** Octaves, sixteenth-note runs into the next chord,
  and it never rests. Written like a lead part, not a foundation — this is the
  single biggest difference from every other four-on-the-floor genre here.
- **Guitar on the sixteenths.** Very short muted chords on every sixteenth, high
  register, low in the mix. The engine's `pluck` voice with a fast decay.
- **Harmony moves and is jazzy.** Minor seventh chords, ii–V motion, real
  cadences. `[i, iv, VII, III]` is the classic minor-disco cycle.
