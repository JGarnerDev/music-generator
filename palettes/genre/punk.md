# Punk

Three chords, played fast, badly, and loud. The kit is deliberately the simplest
in this folder: eighths on the hat, snare on 2 and 4, kick on 1 and 3, forever.
Any cleverness in the drums is a different genre.

## Groove

- **Tempo:** 160–205 BPM. At the top of that range the backbeat starts to read as
  one-per-bar, which is the hardcore feel.
- **Straight, and hit hard.** No swing, no ghost notes, every velocity near the
  top. This is the other genre — with [`techno`](./techno.md) — where humanizing
  is wrong, though for the opposite reason: not machine precision but a drummer
  hitting everything as hard as they can.
- **Power chords only.** Root and fifth, no thirds, which is why `mode: either`
  works: the guitars don't state one and the vocal decides.
- **The whole song is 90 seconds.** Two sections, one repeat, done. A punk loop
  that runs for four minutes has misunderstood.

## Layer it with

[`angry`](../emotion/angry.md) obviously, but
[`happy`](../emotion/happy.md) × punk is pop-punk and is a real and useful cue.
[`desert-fuzz`](../timbre/desert-fuzz.md) for the cheap-amp sound this wants.
