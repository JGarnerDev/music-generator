# Happy / Bright

Use for warm, sunny, feel-good moments — a good morning, a small victory, easy
company. Bright but not manic; lo-fi keeps it grounded.

## Direction

- **Tonality:** major (Ionian). The I–V–vi–IV loop is the workhorse; keep the
  vi so the brightness has a little heart in it.
- **Tempo:** 92–120 BPM. Enough bounce to nod to, not enough to rush.
- **Voicing:** open triads with an added 9th on piano; short plucked upper
  voice for sparkle. Bass on roots, walking on the turnaround.
- **Melody:** arpeggiated, mostly diatonic, favor the 3rd and 6th to stay sweet.
  Rise on phrase openings, settle on the tonic.
- **Dynamics:** medium-bright (velocity 0.6–0.85). Let the pluck pop above the
  piano bed.

## Lo-fi treatment

- Gentle low-pass (~4 kHz) so it's warm, not sterile; light crackle.
- Short slap reverb on the pluck, drier piano. A touch of swing on the hats/
  pluck to keep it human.
