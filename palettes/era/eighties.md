# The Eighties

A period lean rather than a genre or a mood. Unlike the older eras here, this one
is defined almost entirely by **production** rather than by harmony — the chords
are ordinary pop chords, and everything that makes it sound like 1985 is what was
done to them.

## The sound

- **Gated reverb** on the snare: an enormous hall cut off abruptly after a fifth
  of a second. The single most identifiable production choice of the decade, and
  it is a *snare* effect — putting it on everything is a 2010s pastiche of the
  80s rather than the 80s.
- **Chorus on everything else.** Bass, guitar, keys. Width was new and nobody was
  subtle about it.
- **Big, bright, and compressed.** This is the one era palette that fights the
  house lo-fi bed: warmth and a rolled-off top are exactly wrong. Push the
  cutoff up.

## Harmony

Diatonic pop, with the vi–IV–I–V axis doing most of the work. The minor version
(`[i, VI, III, VII]`) is the same four chords and is the sound of every chase
sequence of the period. Sustained pads holding whole notes under a moving bass.

## Rhythm

A drum machine, and it should sound like one: dead straight, no swing, no
humanize. This is the one place in this repo where **not** humanizing is the
authentic choice — the LinnDrum's rigidity is the point.

Four-on-the-floor kick, snare on 2 and 4 with the gate, and a sixteenth hat.
`four-on-floor-stab` for the keys.
