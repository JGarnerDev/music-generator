# Soul

A hard backbeat under warm major harmony. What separates it from pop is the
**ghost notes**: the space between the 2 and the 4 is full of quiet sixteenths on
the hat and snare, and that undergrowth is why a soul groove feels alive at 80
BPM where a plain backbeat feels slow.

## Groove

- **Tempo:** 72–108 BPM. Slow, and never rushed — the genre's whole posture is
  that it has time.
- **Ghosts everywhere.** The `o` hits above are not decoration; take them out and
  this is a rock beat.
- **The bass plays a line.** Walking or half-walking, hitting the root on the
  downbeat and moving through passing tones to the next chord.
- **Borrowed minor iv.** `[I, iii, IV, iv]` — the same chord that makes
  [`nostalgic`](../emotion/nostalgic.md) wistful, used here to ache.

Layer with [`romantic`](../emotion/romantic.md),
[`hopeful`](../emotion/hopeful.md) or [`sad`](../emotion/sad.md), and with
[`rhodes`](../timbre/rhodes.md), which is the sound of the keys part.
