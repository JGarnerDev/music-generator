# Trap

Half-time drums under sixteenth-note hats. The clap lands once a bar rather than
twice, so at 140 BPM the *feel* is 70 — slow, heavy and spacious — while the hats
skitter along at the notated tempo. That contradiction is the genre.

## Groove

- **Tempo:** 130–155 BPM notated, half-time felt. Write the harmony at half
  speed: one chord for a whole bar, often for four.
- **The hats do the work.** Sixteenths with bursts of faster subdivision — the
  runs of `xxxx` above are the "roll", and varying where they land is the main
  rhythmic event in most trap tracks.
- **The 808 is the bass and the kick.** See [`808`](../timbre/808.md): a long,
  gliding, distorted sine that replaces both. The kick lane above is sparse
  precisely so the 808 has room.
- **Minimal harmony.** A minor loop, sometimes literally one chord, with a thin
  high melodic figure over it — bell, music box, detuned pluck.
