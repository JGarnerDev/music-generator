# Calm / Peaceful

Use for rest, safety, downtime — a camp at night, a quiet tavern, a long road
with nothing on it, the exhale after the fight. The most-needed cue in a D&D
session and the easiest to overplay: this music's job is to *not* demand
attention.

## Direction

- **Tonality:** major, but soft — lean on IV and vi rather than a strong V→I
  cadence, so nothing feels like it's arriving. Suspended 2nds and added 9ths
  keep chords open and unresolved-but-content. Avoid leading-tone tension.
- **Tempo:** 56–80 BPM. Slow, but let the *rhythm* be still rather than the notes
  be sparse — a steady gentle pulse is what makes it safe.
- **Voicing:** wide and low-density. Root + 5th + 9th on piano, pad holding whole
  notes underneath, pluck for a slow arpeggio that never hurries. One idea at a
  time; silence between phrases is fine.
- **Melody:** stepwise, small range (a 5th or less), long notes, phrases that
  end where they began. No leaps, no climax — this cue loops without a peak.
- **Dynamics:** soft and even (velocity 0.3–0.55). Almost no swells; a swell is
  an event, and there are no events here.

## Lo-fi treatment

- Warm low-pass (~2 kHz), light vinyl crackle, slow tape wobble — the noise floor
  is the comfort. This is the palette that most rewards `lofi` and `tape`.
- Long wet reverb on the pad, moderately dry piano so it stays intimate. Gentle
  swing (~54%) to take the machine edge off.
