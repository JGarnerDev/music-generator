# Epic / Heroic

Use for triumph, adventure, the swell of a big moment — a hero standing up, a
vista opening. Grand and lifting, but keep it lo-fi-sized, not orchestral bombast.

## Direction

- **Tonality:** major, with the vi giving weight so the triumph earns itself. The
  vi–IV–I–V climb is the classic soaring shape.
- **Tempo:** 88–132 BPM. A steady, marching pulse underneath the swell.
- **Voicing:** wide — bass on roots an octave down, pad sustaining full triads,
  piano doubling the top for shine. Suspensions (4–3) before the resolve add lift.
- **Melody:** leaps of a 4th/5th on phrase openings, long held tonic/5th on the
  peak. Build the contour upward across the loop.
- **Dynamics:** grows (velocity 0.5 → 0.9). Swell the pad into each downbeat.

## Lo-fi treatment

- Keep some highs (~5 kHz low-pass) so it can shine; light crackle to sit it in
  the lo-fi world. Big reverb on the pad, medium on piano. A slow riser/tape
  swell into the first downbeat sells the "epic".
