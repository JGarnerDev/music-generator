# Doo-Wop

The four chords — `I vi IV V`, the "ice cream changes" — under close vocal
harmony, in 12/8 so every beat is a triplet. It is one of the most rigidly
defined idioms here, and the rigidity is the charm.

## Groove

- **24 steps to the bar.** 12/8: four beats, each split in three, written out
  rather than swung. That triplet lope is the period sound, and approximating it
  with `swing` on a 4/4 grid is audibly not the same thing.
- **Tempo:** 56–88 BPM, counted in the four dotted beats. Slow and swaying.
- **Piano triplets.** The right hand plays every one of the twelve subdivisions
  as a chord. It should be relentless; it is the entire accompaniment.
- **Harmony never deviates.** Those four chords, one per bar, round and round.
  Substituting `ii` for `IV` is as adventurous as this gets.

Layer with [`romantic`](../emotion/romantic.md) or
[`nostalgic`](../emotion/nostalgic.md), and with
[`choir`](../timbre/choir.md) for the backing vocals — which in this idiom are
