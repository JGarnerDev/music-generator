# Taste

Rules for composing here, derived from [studies](./studies.md) — attempts at a
musical concept that were played and thumbed up or down.

**This file is the only record.** A study is scratch: it is fanned out, rendered,
judged, distilled into a rule here, and then *deleted* — files and audio both.
So a rule has to stand on its own, in terms of fields and knobs this repo has,
without pointing at a study, a set name, an id or an mp3. If a rule needs an
artifact to be understood, it is not written well enough yet.

What survives from a study is the finding: the concept, the axis, which way the
thumbs went, and what to do about it. That is enough to argue with a rule, and
enough to overturn one — run the same concept and axis again and see whether it
still holds.

## How a rule gets written

A rule goes in when a tag has landed the same way **four or five times** on the
same concept. Fewer than that is an anecdote — a mood on a particular afternoon —
and writing it down would harden a coincidence into a house style, which is
precisely the failure `docs/variety.md` exists about. A rule below that bar can
still be recorded, marked **provisional**, so long as it says so.

Format, one section per rule:

```markdown
### <the rule, as an instruction>

<one paragraph: what to do, concretely, in terms of fields this repo has.>

**From:** `<concept>` × `<axis>` — `<tag>` ×N up / ×M down, <n> attempts.
<which values won and lost.>
```

No filenames, no set names, no ids. Those stop existing.

## Standing preferences

Things stated directly rather than derived from a tally. They are here because
they steer composition the same way a rule does, and they are marked as
unevidenced so a study can later confirm or overturn them.

### Two lead lines, not one

Write leads in pairs rather than a single top line over a bed. Pieces with one
lead have read as thin and directionless. Concretely: two `lead` tracks with
different `pan`. *How* the pair should relate is no longer open — see the twin
leads rule below, which rules out the answering and trading readings this
preference used to offer.

**From:** stated directly. Still unstudied as stated: no set has put one lead
against two, only two leads against each other. The `call-response` concept
exists to test the wider gap.

## Derived rules

### For a chorus lift, commit the register — don't sit in the middle

Take `register` to `subterranean` or `high`. The middle two read as `thin`: `low`
and `mid` fold the roots into the same crowded octave as the pads and pluck, so
the section gets busier without lifting. Pick an extreme and let the gap do the
work.

**From:** `chorus-lift` × `register` — `thin` ×0 up / ×2 down, 8 attempts across
two moods and two keys. Subterranean and high up in both sets, low and mid down
in both — the pattern replicated exactly on a second run, which is what took
this off provisional. The `thin` reading of *why* is still only twice-said, so
the instruction is firmer than the explanation.

### Empty the hole properly — take the harmony out, not just a colour

When a section drops out, cut to one or two parts and bring the whole bed back
at once. Removing a single mid layer while bass and chords keep stating the
harmony was heard as no gap at all — the piece got quieter without opening.
What landed was cutting to bass-and-melody, or to the top line alone, for two
bars.

**From:** `negative-space` × `density` — untagged, 4 attempts. All four parts
down, one-layer-out down, pad-and-chords-out up, melody-alone up: a clean
ladder, emptier winning at every rung. **Provisional** — one set, and the thumbs
carried no tags, so this is evidence that emptier won, not of why.

### Twin leads should lock together, not act independently

Write the second lead as the first one thickened: diatonic thirds or an octave
doubling, sharing the rhythm exactly. Both readings where the two lines were
independent lost — trading bars so they never overlap, and contrary motion. A
second lead earns its place by making one line bigger, not by being a second
thing to follow.

**From:** `twin-leads` × `interplay` — `right-tone` ×2 up / ×0 down, 4 attempts
with note counts held equal so density could not be the thing being judged.
Harmonised in thirds up, octave-doubled up, trading bars down, contrary motion
down. **Provisional** — one set. It narrows the standing preference above rather
than confirming it.

## Worth studying next

Ordered by how much a verdict would change:

- `twin-leads` × `interplay`, a second mood — the newest rule, and the one that
  changes the most pieces if it holds.
- `negative-space` × `density`, a second mood — confirm or kill the ladder.
- `guitar-solo` × `phrasing` — a written axis, so it needs composing by hand,
  and it is the one most likely to produce a rule that changes every piece.
- `hook` × `note-choice` — what makes a cell hummable for this listener.
- `call-response` × `interplay` — the twin-leads verdict went against the two
  lines separating; this asks whether a wider gap between *different*
  instruments fares any better.
