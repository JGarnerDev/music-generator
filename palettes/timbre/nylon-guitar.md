# Nylon-String Guitar

Fingers on nylon: round, warm and quiet, with none of the metallic bite a
plectrum on steel gives. It is the sound of a person playing *to* someone in a
room, which is a different social situation from every other guitar tone here.

## Sound

- **Source:** the `pluck` voice, undriven and soft. The absence of drive is the
  point — one overdriven node and this stops being a nylon guitar.
- **Signal chain:** a touch of compression to even the fingers out → a small
  room. Nothing else. The sparsest chain in this folder on purpose: every effect
  added moves it toward a different instrument.
- **Character:** a strong low-mid body around 200 Hz, a soft top, and a fast
  decay. Notes *stop*; write accordingly, because a nylon guitar cannot sustain a
  pad and pretending it can is the usual mistake.

## When to reach for it

[`flamenco`](../genre/flamenco.md) (with the genre supplying the rasgueado
attack), [`folk`](../genre/folk.md), [`bossa`](../genre/bossa.md),
[`romantic`](../emotion/romantic.md), a fireside scene. Also excellent as a
counter-voice under a piano — it occupies a register the keys leave empty.

## How to voice it

Arpeggiate rather than strum, and use the open strings: the reason guitar music
sits in E, A and D is that those keys let strings ring under the fingered notes.
Keep the bass note and the melody more than an octave apart; the middle is where
this instrument lives and it gets crowded fast.
