# Ambient

A *groove/harmony* layer, not a mood — and the one that barely grooves at all.
Pair with an emotion for key + feeling (`calm` × `ambient` = a safe camp;
`mysterious` × `ambient` = a strange place; `sad` × `ambient` = empty grief).
The best fit for the current engine: no kit is missing, because there is no kit.

## Groove

- **Tempo:** 48–76 BPM — and treat it as a *decay rate*, not a pulse. Nothing
  should land on a beat hard enough to count.
- **Feel:** rhythm is deliberately absent. Notes enter by fading in, not by
  attacking. Let events fall between the grid divisions and overlap the bar line;
  a note that starts late and ends later is correct here.
- **Harmony:** two chords is a full progression. Change once every two or four
  bars, never once a bar. `mode: either` — major floats, minor hollows out; the
  last two progressions are the minor-idiom pair, picked automatically when the
  emotion is minor. Move
  by common tone (I↔vi, I↔IV) so the change is felt rather than heard. Suspended
  and add9 voicings, no leading tones, no cadence — the loop must not arrive.
- **Instrumentation:** pad is the lead instrument, not the bed. Piano and pluck
  are *events* — a single note every few bars, drenched, decaying past the next
  chord. Bass optional and, if present, a held drone on the tonic.
- **Density:** the mistake is adding a second idea. One texture, slowly changing,
  outlasts three stacked ones.
