# Gospel

Church music with a rhythm section. Harmonically the richest thing in this
folder: passing diminished chords, secondary dominants, and a bass that walks
through every one of them. The `[I, III, IV, I]` above is a secondary dominant —
a major III pulling to IV — and it is the single most gospel-sounding move
available.

## Groove

- **Tempo:** 68–116 BPM straight; see [`gospel-shuffle`](./gospel-shuffle.md) for
  the 12/8 triplet feel, which is the other half of the tradition.
- **Clap on the backbeat.** A congregation, doubling the snare. It is the
  cheapest way to make a piece sound like it has people in it.
- **The piano is the lead instrument** and it is busy: full two-handed voicings,
  runs between phrases, and a tremolo'd chord to end a section.
- **Call and response.** Write the melody as a phrase and an answer, with real
  silence between — that gap is where the congregation is.

Layer with [`hopeful`](../emotion/hopeful.md) or
[`solemn`](../emotion/solemn.md), and with [`choir`](../timbre/choir.md) or
[`pipe-organ`](../timbre/pipe-organ.md).
