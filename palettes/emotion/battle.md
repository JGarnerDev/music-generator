# Battle / Combat

Use for active fights, chases, a D&D encounter in full swing — driving, urgent,
relentless. Where `metal` is swagger, this is *motion*: the music has to run.

## Direction

- **Tonality:** minor with a strong V→i pull so each bar resets the drive. The
  iv–V–i cadence gives combat its "clash and recover" pulse.
- **Tempo:** 120–160 BPM. Fast, steady, unforgiving — the heartbeat of the scene.
- **Voicing:** ostinato bass (repeating 8th/16th root pattern) is the engine; keep
  it going. Plucked stabs on off-beats for hits; pad holds tension underneath.
- **Melody:** short, aggressive minor-pentatonic motifs, repeated and sequenced up
  a step to raise stakes. Rhythmic over lyrical.
- **Dynamics:** loud and driving (velocity 0.7–0.95), with accented downbeats.

## Lo-fi treatment

- Keep it punchy — light low-pass only (~5 kHz), some drive on the bass. Tight
  short reverb so hits stay crisp. Lock to the grid; save swing for calmer cues.
  A tape-stop or filter drop works as a section transition when the fight turns.
