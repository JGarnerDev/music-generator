# Angry / Furious

Use for rage, defiance, vengeance, a betrayal landing, a character done being
patient — and for cool-headed swagger, which is anger with the lid on. Where
`battle` is motion and `tense` is fear, this is *pressure*.

## Direction

- **Tonality:** natural minor. The i–VII–VI descent is the seething version;
  i–VI–iv–V builds to a snap. Keep the 5th prominent — hollow, not sweet.
- **Tempo:** 88–140 BPM. Low end = simmering and dangerous; high = boiling over.
- **Voicing:** roots + 5ths, skip the 3rd for grit. Short, clipped notes on bass
  and pluck; piano for low stabs, never a pretty chord. Repetition is the point —
  the same figure refusing to let go.
- **Melody:** minor-pentatonic, low in the register, hammered rather than sung.
  Add the b2 or the tritone for bite. Repeat a motif past comfort, then break it.
- **Dynamics:** loud and flat (velocity 0.7–0.95). Restraint at low tempo reads
  as menace; keep it *controlled* until the release.

## Lo-fi treatment

- Drive and light bitcrush on bass/pluck. Mid-forward EQ — this needs bite, so
  scoop nothing. Short, tight room reverb; stay dry and close.
- Lock to the grid, no swing. A filter slam or tape-stop marks the moment the
  anger breaks.
