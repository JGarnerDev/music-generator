# Brown Sound (Van Halen)

A *sound* — Eddie's saturated-but-woody guitar tone. A timbre only: apply it to an
`angry`/`battle` emotion plus the `metal` (or a future `rock`) genre for the
notes and feel.

## Sound

- **Source:** a distorted "guitar" voice (use the pluck instrument, driven).
  Power-chord-friendly — roots + 5ths, palm-muted attack.
- **Signal chain:** lower the supply voltage (variac sag → softer, browner
  distortion) → overdrive into saturation → plate reverb → a short slap echo for
  the room. Order matters: sag before drive.
- **Character:** thick and warm, not fizzy or scooped. Mid-forward so it cuts;
  compressed sustain that blooms. Heavy but musical — woody, never harsh.
