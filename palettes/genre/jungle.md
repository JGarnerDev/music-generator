# Jungle

A subtype of [`dnb`](./dnb.md) — that file layers first and supplies the tempo
band, the modal minor harmony and the sub bass; this states only the drums,
because the drums are the entire difference.

Where drum & bass programmes a break, jungle **chops a sampled one**: the Amen
break cut into sixteenths and reassembled out of order, so the snare lands in
places no drummer would put it and the kick arrives late on purpose. The pattern
above is deliberately asymmetrical across its two bars — that irregularity is
what the ear identifies, and tidying it up produces drum & bass.

## Groove

- **Two-bar patterns minimum**, and the two bars must differ. A one-bar jungle
  break is a contradiction in terms.
- **Ghost notes everywhere.** The `o` hits are half the sound: the break was
  played by a human at low volume between the loud hits, and losing them leaves
  a drum machine.
- **Ride or hat, not both busy.** The break already carries the subdivision.

Pairs with [`tense`](../emotion/tense.md) and
[`angry`](../emotion/angry.md); layer [`tape`](../timbre/tape.md) for the
sampled-off-vinyl quality the whole genre came from.
