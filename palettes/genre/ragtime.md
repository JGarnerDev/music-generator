# Ragtime

A march in the left hand, syncopation in the right, and the tension between them
is the whole style. No drum kit — the left hand *is* the rhythm section, which is
why this palette states no groove.

## How it works

- **Left hand:** bass note on beats 1 and 3, chord on 2 and 4. Oom-pah, strictly
  in time, never syncopated. Use `four-on-floor-stab` or write it as a two-voice
  part.
- **Right hand:** accents on the off-beats, tied across the beat, deliberately at
  odds with the left. The `3+3+2` figure is the closest cell on the shelf.
- **Harmony:** functional and busy — secondary dominants everywhere, a `I7`
  pulling to IV, a diminished passing chord into every cadence. Sixteen-bar
  strains, each repeated, in the key or its subdominant.
- **Never swing it.** Ragtime is played straight; the swung reading is jazz and is
  about twenty years later.

Layer with [`whimsical`](../emotion/whimsical.md) or
[`happy`](../emotion/happy.md), a saloon, a chase in a silent film. Pair with
[`tavern`](../space/tavern.md) and an out-of-tune upright.
