# Analog Synth

A *sound*, not a mood or a groove. Layer it onto any emotion/genre to recolor the
voices — it carries no key, tempo, or progression of its own.

## Sound

- **Source:** two or three saw oscillators, slightly detuned (a few cents apart)
  for width and beating. Sub-oscillator an octave down for weight.
- **Signal chain:** resonant low-pass with a slow envelope/LFO sweep (the classic
  filter movement) → light chorus for width → tape echo for depth. In order.
- **Character:** warm, round, a little unstable (pitch drift = analog). Bright
  when the filter opens, dark and pillowy when closed. Never sterile or digital.
