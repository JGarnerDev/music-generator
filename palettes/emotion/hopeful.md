# Hopeful / Uplifting

Use for gentle optimism, healing, a sunrise, "it's going to be okay." Warmer and
more tender than `happy`, lifting rather than celebrating — the near neighbor of
`sad` turned toward the light.

## Direction

- **Tonality:** major with the iii for wistfulness before the IV–V lift. Suspended
  chords (sus2/sus4) resolving upward carry the "hope."
- **Tempo:** 72–104 BPM. A breathing, unhurried pace.
- **Voicing:** warm mid-register piano with added 9ths; pad underneath swelling
  into each chord. Light pluck for a hopeful upper sparkle, used sparingly.
- **Melody:** stepwise and rising, phrases that lift to the 3rd or 5th and settle
  gently — not a hard cadence, a soft landing.
- **Dynamics:** soft, growing (velocity 0.45–0.75). Let the pad bloom.

## Lo-fi treatment

- Warm low-pass (~3.5 kHz), light crackle, gentle wobble. Medium-long reverb on
  pad and pluck; keep piano fairly close so it stays intimate and sincere.
