# Bossa Nova

Samba played very quietly by four people in a small room. The tempo is quick and
the *feel* is not, because everything is understated — no backbeat, no crescendo,
and a singer who never raises their voice.

## Groove

- **No snare.** The rim clave above carries the whole rhythm, and it is a two-bar
  cell that does not repeat within the bar. Adding a backbeat turns this into
  lounge music instantly.
- **Tempo:** 112–148 BPM, felt in two.
- **Harmony is the point.** Major sevenths, ninths, a major II pulling to ii
  (`[I, II, ii, V]` — a secondary dominant, and the sound of the genre). Every
  chord has at least four notes and none of them is a plain triad.
- **The guitar plays chord and bass together**, thumb on the root, fingers on the
  off-beats. [`nylon-guitar`](../timbre/nylon-guitar.md) is not optional.
