# Spaghetti Western

A *groove/harmony* layer, not a mood. Named in
[`docs/vision.md`](../../docs/vision.md) as the most-specific concept worth
storing — anything narrower gets derived in conversation. Pair with an emotion
(`tense` × `spaghetti-western` = the standoff; `lonely` × it = the long ride;
`battle` × it = the draw).

## Groove

- **Tempo:** 72–116 BPM. The standoff is slow and *stretched*; the chase gallops
  at the top of the range with a triplet feel.
- **Feel:** wide open. Long silences between gestures — the genre is mostly
  space, punctuated. When it moves, it moves in a galloping triplet or a
  march-like quarter pulse.
- **Harmony:** minor, with the **Andalusian descent** (i–VII–VI–V) as the
  signature — that stepwise fall to a strong V is the sound. Hold the V long; the
  unresolved dominant *is* the tension of the scene. Bare 5ths, no extensions.
- **Instrumentation:** twangy pluck as the lead guitar with lots of reverb, pad
  standing in for the wordless choir or whistle, bass on sparse roots, piano for
  a single tolling note (the bell). One voice at a time — this genre is a
  conversation between soloists, never a band playing together.
- **Melody:** a whistled tune. Small range, long notes, wide vibrato implied by
  slow trills. State it, leave a huge gap, answer it an octave down.
