# Folk / Trad

A *groove/harmony* layer, not a mood. Pair with an emotion for key + feeling
(`calm` × `folk` = the hearth after the road; `hopeful` × `folk` = the setting
out; `sad` × `folk` = the ballad about who didn't come back). This is the D&D
downtime genre — village, tavern, travel montage.

**Modal by default.** Set the emotion's `scale: dorian` when you want the real
thing: dorian is minor with a *natural* 6th, so the IV chord comes out major
while the tonic stays minor — that lift is the whole sound, and it's why this
genre reads as old rather than sad. Against a plain `minor` emotion the
progressions below still borrow a major IV (numerals resolve as written), but
the melody keeps drawing on the flat 6 and the two rub; prefer a dorian emotion.
A `major` emotion picks the third progression and lands mixolydian — the other
trad colour, and equally correct here.

## Groove

- **Tempo:** 84–132 BPM. A ballad sits at the bottom; a reel drives at the top
  and wants the shaker doubled rather than the kit made busier.
- **Feel:** a bodhrán, not a drum kit — a steady eighth pulse on the low tom with
  the accent on the downbeat, a rim on 2 and 4, shaker filling the gaps. Straight,
  no swing. The energy comes from the strumming, not the percussion.
- **Harmony:** three chords, one per bar, no extensions — open triads with a
  doubled 5th read as a drone. Movement is stepwise (i–VII–IV), never a
  functional V–i cadence; a leading tone would make it classical. Let the tonic
  ring underneath as a pedal where you can.
- **Instrumentation:** pluck is the strummed guitar/mandolin/bouzouki, piano
  carries the tune (a fiddle stand-in until sampled instruments land), pad is
  the drone/pipes, bass on plain roots.
- **Melody:** a singable tune with a small range that repeats its phrase with a
  different ending — the trad AABB shape. Ornament by repeating a note quickly
  rather than by adding chromatics.
- **Gated on meter:** the jig (6/8) and the slip jig (9/8) are the other half of
  this genre and need time-signature support (see progress.md). What's here is
  the reel/ballad side, in 4/4.
