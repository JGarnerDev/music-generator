# Nostalgic / Wistful

A remembered thing, and the knowledge that it is remembered. The tavern you grew
up in, seen twenty years later; a theme from act one played slowly in act three.
The defining move is that the music is **major and still sad**, which is what
separates it from [`sad`](./sad.md) — nothing is wrong, it is simply gone.

## Direction

- **Tonality:** major, with the minor iv borrowed from the parallel minor
  (`[I, IV, iv, I]`). That chord is the whole palette: it turns a bright
  progression wistful without changing key, exactly the way a memory turns a
  good day into a loss. Use iii where you'd use I, so the tonic keeps slipping.
- **Tempo:** 64–86 BPM, unhurried but walking. Slower than this becomes a lament.
- **Voicing:** mid-register and slightly cluttered — added 9ths, a held pedal
  tone underneath a moving line. The pedal is the past; the line is now.
- **Melody:** simple and singable, like a tune someone half-remembers, and then
  *not quite* repeated the second time. The variation is the point: memory is
  inaccurate, and a phrase that comes back changed says so.
- **Dynamics:** 0.3–0.6, softening at phrase ends rather than swelling.

## Lo-fi treatment

- This is the palette [`tape`](../timbre/tape.md) exists for. Wow and flutter,
  the top rolled off at 2–3 kHz, the noise floor audible. A clean render of this
  palette is a worse render of it.
- Quoting a [leitmotif](../../docs/library.md) here is worth doing on purpose —
  a theme from earlier in a campaign, transposed and slowed, is nostalgia stated
  literally rather than described. `npm run motif:quote`.

## Layer it with

- [`lofi`](../genre/lofi.md) for the obvious and correct reading.
- [`jazz`](../genre/jazz.md) for something more adult and less sentimental.
- [`romantic`](./romantic.md) if the memory is of a person rather than a place.
