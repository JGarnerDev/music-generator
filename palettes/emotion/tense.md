# Tense / Dread

Use for suspense, creeping horror, the moment before something goes wrong. The
feeling is *withheld* — energy coiled, not released.

## Direction

- **Tonality:** natural minor, leaning on the i and the pull of V back to i. For
  extra unease, hint Phrygian by leaning a melody note on the b2 over the i
  (theory stays minor-diatonic so progressions still resolve).
- **Tempo:** 64–92 BPM. A slow pulse reads as dread; a faster one as chase.
- **Voicing:** hold a low drone on bass + pad; move as little as possible. Sparse
  piano stabs on off-beats. Dissonance from held 2nds against the root.
- **Melody:** narrow range, chromatic neighbor tones, unresolved phrases. Let
  notes hang without landing on the tonic until the very end.
- **Dynamics:** quiet and swelling (velocity 0.3–0.6), with sudden accents.
  Volume that creeps up sells the tension.

## Lo-fi treatment

- Dark low-pass (~1.8 kHz), heavier tape wobble for instability. Long reverb
  tail on the pad, near-dry piano stabs so hits feel close and sharp.
