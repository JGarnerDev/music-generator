# Sad / Bittersweet

Use for loss, farewells, quiet grief, a friend or pet dying, tender endings. The
goal is *moving*, not merely slow — restraint carries the feeling.

## Direction

- **Tonality:** natural minor (Aeolian). For a bittersweet lift, borrow the major
  VI or a Picardy third on the final chord.
- **Tempo:** 60–78 BPM. Let notes ring; silence is part of the phrase.
- **Voicing:** sparse. Root + a suspended or added 9th on piano; long pad
  underneath. Avoid dense triads on every beat.
- **Melody:** stepwise, descending phrases resolve to the tonic. Leave gaps.
- **Dynamics:** soft (velocity 0.4–0.7). Swell the pad; keep piano intimate.

## Lo-fi treatment

- Warm low-pass (~2.5 kHz), light vinyl crackle, gentle tape wobble.
- Long, wet reverb on the pad; drier piano so it stays close.
