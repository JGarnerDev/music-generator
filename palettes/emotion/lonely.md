# Lonely / Desolate

Nobody is coming. A dead village, a frozen pass, the long walk after everyone
else is gone. Not [`sad`](./sad.md), which is grief and therefore about
something that happened — this is about *absence*, and absence has no event in
it.

## Direction

- **Tonality:** natural minor, no leading tone at all. The major V is the sound
  of somewhere to go; write `v` and it stays hollow. Fifths and octaves rather
  than triads — leaving out the third is the single most effective thing in this
  palette, because a chord with no third has no opinion.
- **Tempo:** 48–68 BPM, and the *harmony* slower still: one chord for two bars,
  sometimes four. A progression that keeps moving is company.
- **Voicing:** extreme registers with nothing in the middle. A very low pad and a
  very high single line, and the gap between them is the whole idea. Never fill
  it in.
- **Melody:** one short phrase, repeated with long silences between. It should
  sound like it is being played to nobody, so it need not develop; a tune that
  develops is a tune with an audience.
- **Dynamics:** 0.25–0.45. Quiet, and — unlike `calm` — *uneven*, so the odd
  louder note reads as the only sound for miles rather than as a swell.

## Lo-fi treatment

- Long, dark reverb with a high wet mix: the reverb is the landscape, and it
  should be more present than the source. This palette wants
  [`palettes/space/cave`](../space/cave.md) or `open-field` more than most.
- Roll the top off hard. Brightness is presence, and there is nobody here.

## Layer it with

- [`ambient`](../genre/ambient.md) — drumless, which is correct: a beat implies a
  body moving, and there is no body.
- If a scene needs a pulse anyway, keep the kit to a slow rim or a shaker and
  drop the snare entirely.
