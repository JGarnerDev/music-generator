# Music Box

A rotating cylinder plucking a steel comb. Thin, glassy, entirely without low
end, and slightly out of tune with itself. Two scenes want it, and they are
opposites: a child's bedroom, and something very wrong in a child's bedroom.

## Sound

- **Source:** the `pluck` voice, very short decay, no sustain at all. Every note
  is an attack and a fast ring-off.
- **Signal chain:** a high-pass at 300 Hz or above — a music box has *no*
  fundamental, and leaving one in is what makes a fake one sound like a
  glockenspiel → a band limit → a small room. It is a small object; give it a
  small space.
- **Character:** brittle, silvery, mechanically even. The unevenness that makes it
  eerie is in the *tuning*, not the timing.

## When to reach for it

[`whimsical`](../emotion/whimsical.md), a lullaby, or
[`mysterious`](../emotion/mysterious.md) / [`tense`](../emotion/tense.md) when you
want a horror cue nobody can accuse you of overplaying. Pair it with
`meter: [3, 4]` and the `waltz-oom-pah` figure for the full wind-up-toy effect.

## How to voice it

One line, two octaves above middle C, and no chords — a comb plays one note at a
time and stacking them destroys the illusion instantly. If a scene needs harmony
underneath, that is another instrument's job. Let the tune slow and stop rather
than fade: a box runs down.
