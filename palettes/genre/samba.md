# Samba / Batucada

The surdo — the big low drum — on beat **two**, not one. That single displacement
is samba: the heaviest sound in the ensemble lands where a rock drummer would put
the backbeat, and the downbeat is left to be implied by everything else.

## Groove

- **Tempo:** 96–132 BPM.
- **Count in two, not four.** Samba is felt as a fast 2/4; the sixteenth shaker is
  the surface and the surdo is the pulse underneath it.
- **The rim plays a clave-like cell** that does not line up with the bar, and
  moving it is how the groove develops.
- **Harmony is Brazilian, not Latin-generic.** Minor with a strong V–i, or the
  ii–V vocabulary of [`bossa`](./bossa.md), which is the same music at a whisper.
