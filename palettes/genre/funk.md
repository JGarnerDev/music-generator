# Funk

A *groove* layer. Funk is rhythm-first — harmony often sits on a single vamp
while the pocket does the work. Pair with an emotion for the emotional color.

## Groove

- **Tempo:** 96–116 BPM. The "one" is king — accent beat 1 hard.
- **Feel:** 16th-note syncopation, tight and dry, everything locked to the grid
  with a hair of swing. Space is part of the groove; mute and stab.
- **Harmony:** static — vamp on i (often a dominant-7 #9 flavor). Movement is
  rhythmic, not harmonic; a quick iv or VII stab at most. `mode: minor`.
- **Instrumentation:** slap/finger bass carrying the riff, muted plucked
  "guitar" chanks on the off-beats, electric piano stabs.
