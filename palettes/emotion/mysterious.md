# Mysterious / Enigmatic

Use for wonder, secrets, magic, a strange place that invites you in — curious more
than fearful (that's `tense`). Floating and ambiguous.

## Direction

- **Tonality:** minor, but keep the tonic unconfirmed — pivot through III and VII
  so the ear never fully settles. Whole-tone or Dorian *color* in the melody adds
  the "which key is this?" feel; the chords stay minor-diatonic.
- **Tempo:** 70–100 BPM. Unhurried, with space for notes to shimmer.
- **Voicing:** open 5ths and add-2 chords (no strong 3rds) so quality stays
  ambiguous. Sparse plucked bell-tones high; pad low and wide.
- **Melody:** wandering, non-repeating, wide intervals. Avoid clear cadences —
  drift rather than resolve.
- **Dynamics:** soft and even (velocity 0.4–0.65), gentle swells.

## Lo-fi treatment

- Mid low-pass (~3 kHz) with a slow filter sweep for movement. Long, wide reverb
  and a touch of delay on the pluck (bell echoes). Subtle wobble for the dreamlike
  drift.
