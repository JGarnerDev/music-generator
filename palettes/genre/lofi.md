# Lo-Fi Hip-Hop

A *groove/harmony* layer, not a mood — and the house style of this repo. Pair
with an emotion for key + feeling (`calm` × `lofi` = study beats; `sad` × `lofi`
= a rainy-window loop; `nostalgic` × `lofi` = the default sound of the genre).
Stack `tape` on top for the dust.

## Groove

- **Tempo:** 70–90 BPM. The whole genre lives here; 82 is the safe centre. Slow
  enough to feel unhurried, fast enough to nod to.
- **Feel:** boom-bap at half-time — kick on 1 and the *and* of 2, snare on 3.
  Swung 8ths (55–62%) and, more importantly, **loose**: nudge hats and keys a few
  ms off the grid. The imperfection is the genre. Bass plays sparse root motion
  on the downbeats, behind the beat.
- **Harmony:** jazz vocabulary at walking pace — ii–V–I, extended to 7ths and
  9ths everywhere (a plain triad sounds wrong here). `mode: either`: major reads
  hazy-warm, minor reads rainy. Keep changes slow, one or two chords per bar,
  and loop 2–4 bars without resolving hard.
- **Instrumentation:** electric piano (Rhodes) comping is the core voice, upright
  or soft synth bass, a pad way down in the mix, sampled-sounding piano for the
  melody. Fewer voices than you think — space is an instrument.
- **Melody:** short, sung-not-shredded phrases with long rests. Repetition over
  development; the loop should survive twenty passes.
