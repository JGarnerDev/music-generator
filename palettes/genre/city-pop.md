# City Pop

Japanese pop of the early eighties: American session-musician funk played with
jazz harmony and enormous production budgets. Distinct from
[`synthwave`](./synthwave.md), which is a modern imitation of the same decade —
this is the real thing, and it is *major*, busy, and expensively played.

## Groove

- **Tempo:** 98–122 BPM, with a light sixteenth swing.
- **The bass is slapped and busy**, sixteenths and octaves, and it is mixed loud.
- **Harmony is jazz.** `IVmaj7 → III → vi` is the characteristic turn: a major
  seventh, then a *major* chord on the third degree resolving down. Nothing here
  is a plain triad, and a progression that is all triads is not this genre.
- **Everything is doubled.** Guitar and Rhodes on the same chords, bass and
  synth-bass on the same line, and a brass stab answering the vocal.
