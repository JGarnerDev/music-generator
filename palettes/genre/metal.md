# Metal

A *groove/harmony* layer, not a mood. Pair with an emotion for key + feeling
(`angry` × `metal` = fury; `battle` × `metal` = a boss fight; `tense` × `metal` =
a monster closing in). Add `brown-sound` for the tone.

## Groove

- **Tempo:** 96–160 BPM. Mid = headbang; high = thrash urgency.
- **Feel:** hard, even, on the grid — no swing. Palm-muted 8ths/16ths on the low
  strings as the engine; accents on the downbeat and the syncopated push before
  it. Consistency reads as force.
- **Harmony:** power chords — roots + 5ths, the 3rd omitted, so major/minor is
  carried by the melody instead. `mode: minor`. The Aeolian i–VII–VI descent is
  the signature: whole-step root motion reads as heavy. Tritone and b2 for
  menace; pedal the tonic under moving upper voices.
- **Instrumentation:** driven pluck as the guitar, bass doubling the riff an
  octave down, piano only for low stabs or a clean-arpeggio breakdown.
- **Dynamics:** loud and flat (velocity 0.7–0.95). Dynamic range is the enemy;
  contrast comes from arrangement (drop out, come back), not from volume.
