# Drum & Bass

A chopped breakbeat at 170 BPM over a bassline moving at half that speed. The
**tempo split** is the genre: the drums are frantic and the bass and harmony are
slow, so the same track is simultaneously urgent and calm. Nothing else here
does that.

## Groove

- **Tempo:** 165–180 BPM, but write the *harmony* as though it were 85 — one
  chord every two bars, and a bass note that lasts a bar.
- **The snare moves.** A two-bar pattern with the second bar displaced is the
  whole trick; a one-bar snare loop at this tempo is a machine gun. Note the
  ghost snare before the last hit above — that stutter is the "roll".
- **Sub bass, not bass.** Below 80 Hz, sine-heavy, very few notes. See
  [`808`](../timbre/808.md), which applies directly.
- **Minor, and modal rather than functional.** No cadences; the harmony is
  colour, and the drums are the event.

## Layer it with

[`tense`](../emotion/tense.md) for neurofunk, [`calm`](../emotion/calm.md) or
[`nostalgic`](../emotion/nostalgic.md) for liquid — which sounds like a
contradiction and is the best thing this genre does.
[`jungle`](./jungle.md) is the older, breakier subtype.
