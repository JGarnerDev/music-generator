# Metal / Badass

Use for swagger, menace, a cool-under-pressure entrance, a fight you're going to
win. Dark and driving — attitude over prettiness. Lo-fi keeps it grimy, not shiny.

## Direction

- **Tonality:** natural minor with the i–VII–VI Aeolian descent — the "power"
  cadence. Root-motion by whole steps reads as heavy.
- **Tempo:** 96–140 BPM. A hard, even pulse; syncopate the bass for push.
- **Voicing:** power-chord feel — roots + 5ths (skip the 3rd for grit) on bass
  and pluck. Palm-muted-style short notes; piano only for occasional low stabs.
- **Melody:** minor-pentatonic riffs, repeated motifs, bends implied by chromatic
  approach. Keep it low and mean.
- **Dynamics:** loud and flat (velocity 0.7–0.95). Consistency = force.

## Lo-fi treatment

- Bitcrush and drive on the bass/pluck for grit; mid-forward EQ (scoop nothing,
  this needs bite). Short room reverb, tight and dry. Add swing sparingly — this
  wants to hit on the grid.
